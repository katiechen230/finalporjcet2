History of Honolulu

The History Section

Honolulu became an important port during the early 1800s because ships stopped there while crossing the Pacific Ocean. In 1845 it became the capital of the Kingdom of Hawaii. Today it is the largest city in Hawaii and a center for business, tourism, and Hawaiian culture.

The Expert Section

  • Diamond Head volcano formed about 300,000 years ago.
  • Waikiki was once home to Hawaiian royalty.
  • Pearl Harbor is located near Honolulu and is an important historic site.
